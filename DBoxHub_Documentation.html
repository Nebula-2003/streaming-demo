01

Project Summary

Problem Statement

Sports enthusiasts looking to book Box Cricket or Pickleball courts face a fragmented, manual process — phone calls, WhatsApp messages, and in-person visits with no real-time slot visibility. Venue operators struggle with manual booking registers, overbooking conflicts, and no consolidated dashboard for managing multiple courts or staff. There is no dedicated, India-first platform for niche indoor/box sports booking.

Solution Overview

DBoxHub is a unified booking platform that connects customers to nearby sports venues through a mobile app, while empowering venue operators with a dedicated management interface. A master admin panel provides platform-wide oversight of venues, courts, categories, and users.

For Customers

  • Discover nearby venues on a map
  • Browse courts, pricing & availability
  • Book multiple time slots in one go
  • Receive confirmation & reminders
  • Manage and cancel bookings

For Venue Operators

  • Manage courts, slots & pricing
  • Create manual bookings
  • View revenue & utilization
  • Staff access with restricted permissions
  • Real-time booking notifications

Target Users

  • Customers: Sports enthusiasts aged 18–45 looking to book Box Cricket or Pickleball courts
  • Venue Owners: Individual or corporate owners of sports facilities managing one or more venues
  • Venue Staff: Receptionists, managers, or ground staff who handle day-to-day bookings
  • Super Admin: DBoxHub internal operations team managing the entire platform

Key Differentiators

  • Niche focus on Box Cricket and Pickleball (extendable to other sports)
  • Multi-slot booking in a single transaction
  • Single app with role-aware UI for owners, staff, and customers
  • Real-time slot availability with geo-based discovery
  • Venue owner manual booking capability

Suggested Tagline

"Book Your Court. Own Your Game."

Alt: "Every Game Starts with a Booking." · "Your Sport. Your Slot. Your Hub."

Naming & Terminology

💡
Recommendation Use "Venue" instead of "Vendor". "Venue" is the standard industry term for a sports booking platform (used by Playo, Hudle, CourtReserve), accurately represents a physical location, and pairs naturally with "Court". Avoid "Facility" (too clinical), "Club" (implies membership), "Arena" (suggests large-scale events).
Term Definition Notes
Venue A physical sports facility location with an address, geo-coordinates, and operating hours. Owned by a Venue Owner. Can contain multiple Courts. Replaces "Vendor"
Court A specific playable area within a Venue (e.g., "Court A", "Box 1"). Belongs to one Sports Category. Has its own pricing, slot duration, and capacity. Also called "Ground" or "Box"
Sports Category The type of sport a court is designated for (e.g., Box Cricket, Pickleball). Admin-managed and extendable.
Time Slot A defined booking window for a court (e.g., 6:00–7:00 AM). Generated based on court's operating hours and slot duration configuration. Master config per court
Booking A confirmed reservation made by a Customer (or manually by a Venue Owner/Staff) for one or more Time Slots on a specific Court and date. Has status: pending / confirmed / cancelled
Booking Slot A junction record linking a Booking to a specific Time Slot. Supports multi-slot bookings (one booking → many booking_slots). Child of Booking
Venue Owner A user role with full management rights for their assigned Venue(s): courts, slots, bookings, staff, and revenue. role = venue_owner
Venue User / Staff A sub-user created under a Venue Owner. Can manage bookings but cannot access revenue or venue settings. role = venue_user
Customer An end user who discovers venues, books courts, and manages their own bookings through the mobile app. role = customer
Super Admin Internal DBoxHub platform administrator with full access to all modules via the Master Admin Panel. role = super_admin

Authentication Strategy

JWT-based stateless authentication with short-lived access tokens (15 min) and long-lived refresh tokens (30 days) stored in HTTP-only cookies (web) or SecureStore (mobile).

RoleToken ClaimLogin Method
super_adminrole: "super_admin"Email + Password
venue_ownerrole: "venue_owner", venue_idEmail + Password
venue_userrole: "venue_user", venue_idEmail + Password
customerrole: "customer"Phone OTP or Email

API Design

  • RESTful, versioned at /api/v1/
  • JSON request/response bodies
  • Standard HTTP status codes (200, 201, 400, 401, 403, 404, 409, 500)
  • Pagination via ?page=1&limit=20
  • Geo-filtering via ?lat=23.02&lng=72.57&radius=10
  • Error format: {"success": false, "error": {"code": "SLOT_UNAVAILABLE", "message": "..."}}

Feature Specifications

Multi-Slot Booking

User Story

As a Customer, I want to select multiple consecutive or non-consecutive time slots for a court in a single booking, so I can play for longer without making separate transactions.

Acceptance Criteria

  • Customer can select 1–N available slots on a date
  • Total amount is calculated as sum of individual slot prices
  • All selected slots must be available (not already booked)
  • Slot availability is checked in a transaction to prevent race conditions
  • Booking confirmation is sent immediately after success

Business Rules

  • Maximum 6 slots per single booking
  • Slots must be on the same court and same date
  • If any slot in the batch is unavailable, the entire booking fails
  • Cancelled slots become immediately available for rebooking
⚠️
Assumption Max 6 slots per booking. Adjust based on business requirement.

Manual Booking (Owner/Staff)

User Story

As a Venue Owner/Staff, I want to create a booking on behalf of a walk-in or phone customer, so I can record it in the system without the customer needing the app.

Acceptance Criteria

  • Owner/Staff can search for an existing customer by phone or create a guest entry
  • Can select court, date, and one or more slots
  • Booking is flagged as is_manual: true
  • The created_by field records the staff member
  • Customer receives notification if they have the app

Cancellation Policy

Business Rules

  • Customer can cancel up to 2 hours before slot start time
  • Owner/Staff can cancel any booking at any time
  • Super Admin can cancel any booking
  • Cancellation notifications are sent to all relevant parties
  • Cancelled slots are immediately released for rebooking
⚠️
Assumption 2-hour cancellation window assumed. Venue owners may want to configure this per venue in a future milestone.

Venue Discovery (Nearby)

User Story

As a Customer, I want to see sports venues near my location on a map and list view, so I can find the most convenient place to play.

Acceptance Criteria

  • App requests location permission on first use
  • Venues displayed within 10km radius (configurable)
  • Filterable by sports category
  • Shows venue name, distance, rating (future), starting price
  • Tapping a venue opens venue detail page
💡
Recommendation Use PostGIS ST_DWithin with a spatial index on venues.geolocation for efficient geo queries. Index: CREATE INDEX venues_geo_idx ON venues USING GIST(geolocation);
